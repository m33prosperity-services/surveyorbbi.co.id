Primary Activities

Survey services for marine, cargo, bulk, agriculture, laboratory, and insurance work.

BBI helps customers meet operational requirements, protect interests, and produce trustworthy survey report results.

Marine Survey

Towing and lashing, lashing survey, on/off hire vessel survey, bunker survey, hold cleanliness, draft survey, investigation, stowage, and stability.

Cargo Survey

Tally loading and unloading for break bulk cargo and containers, condition or damage survey, measuring of shipping cargo, pre-loading, weighing, and sampling.

Bulk Survey

Hatch cleanliness, hose test, pre-bulk, weighing, sampling, and bunkering support for bulk cargo operations.

Agri Product Survey

Loading and discharging supervision, pre-shipment survey, sampling, analytical review, and testing laboratory coordination.

Independent Laboratory

FOSFA-oriented laboratory service capability with equipment for palm oil product testing and field sample support.

Insurance Survey

Physical general condition survey, condition and damage survey for vessel hull and machinery, and valuation support.

Workflow

From nomination to report, the work stays traceable.

  1. 01Scope confirmation and survey nomination.
  2. 02Field attendance, supervision, measurement, inspection, or sampling.
  3. 03Evidence review, technical verification, and report preparation.
  4. 04Final report delivery for operational or commercial decision making.
